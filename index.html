The Delta Flow framework

A practical way to learn GEO and AEO—without chasing shortcuts.

Working principle

Make the page valuable before making it visible.

Sustainable visibility begins with solving a real problem clearly. Optimisation should help search systems understand the same useful information that people see.

“Optimise for understanding, evidence, usability, and trust—not a promised AI mention.”
01

Map questions and intent

Document what a buyer asks before, during, and after choosing a service. Group questions by intent, urgency, and context.

02

Publish a clear answer system

Create pages that define your offer, explain your method, show constraints, and answer follow-up questions in plain language.

03

Strengthen evidence and entities

Keep brand details consistent, cite reliable sources where appropriate, and add authentic examples, authorship, and business information.

04

Measure, learn, and improve

Use Search Console, page testing, query research, and periodic content reviews to improve what users and search systems can discover.

FAQs

Questions worth answering clearly.

What are GEO and AEO?

GEO—Generative Engine Optimisation—focuses on making useful brand information easier for generative AI systems to understand and use when relevant. AEO—Answer Engine Optimisation— focuses on structuring content to answer user questions directly across modern search experiences.

Does GEO guarantee that ChatGPT, Google AI, or Perplexity will cite a website?

No. Visibility in any search result or AI-generated answer cannot be guaranteed. Ethical optimisation improves clarity, quality, technical accessibility, and evidence—not a promised placement.

What is Delta Flow currently building?

Delta Flow is an independent learning lab focused on practical research into content systems, entity clarity, structured data, technical SEO, and AI-search discoverability.

Why does structured data matter?

Structured data gives search engines standardised context about what a page contains. It should always match the visible content and must never be used to mislead users or search engines.
