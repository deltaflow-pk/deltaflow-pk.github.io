Questions we get asked

What are GEO and AEO?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) makes your brand easier for AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude to understand and mention. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) structures your content so it can be used as a direct answer to a question.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO aims to rank pages in a list of search results. GEO and AEO aim to make your brand and answers part of the AI-written response itself, which depends more on clear answers, consistent brand information and trusted mentions across the web.

Can you guarantee that ChatGPT, Perplexity or Claude will mention my brand?

No. No one can guarantee placement in an AI-generated answer. We improve the things within your control: clarity, structure, evidence, consistency and trusted mentions, and we test your brand in AI tools before and after.

How much does a GEO and AEO project cost?

Pricing depends on the size of your website and the scope of work, so we quote each project individually. Email us your website and goals and we will reply with a clear quote after a short review.

How long does it take to see results?

On-page and profile changes are usually completed in the first few weeks. AI tools update on their own schedule, so we re-test after two to four weeks and report what changed. We do not promise a specific date for AI mentions.

What does a GEO and AEO project include?

It starts with an AI visibility audit, then covers answer-first content, FAQ and structured data, brand consistency across profiles, citation building on trusted platforms, and re-testing in AI tools.
