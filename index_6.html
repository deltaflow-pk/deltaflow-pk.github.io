What are GEO and AEO?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the practice of making your brand clear, consistent and trusted enough that AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude understand it and mention it in their answers.

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is the practice of writing and structuring content so it can be used as the direct answer to a specific question.

Questions we get asked

What are GEO and AEO?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) makes your brand easier for AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude to understand and mention. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) structures your content so it can be used as a direct answer to a question.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO aims to rank pages in a list of search results. GEO and AEO aim to make your brand and answers part of the AI-written response itself, which depends more on clear answers, consistent brand information and trusted mentions across the web.

Can you guarantee that ChatGPT, Perplexity or Claude will mention my brand?

No. No one, including Delta Flow, can guarantee that ChatGPT, Perplexity or Claude will mention a brand. We improve what is within your control: clarity, structure, evidence, consistency and trusted mentions. We also test your brand in these AI tools before and after the work to measure what changed.
