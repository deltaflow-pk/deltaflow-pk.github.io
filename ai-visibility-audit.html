What is an AI visibility audit?

An AI visibility audit tests what AI tools such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude say about your brand and your category, shows which competitors they mention instead, and lists what to fix so your brand is easier to understand and verify.

What we check

Whether you appear

We ask real customer questions about your category in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude and record whether your brand is mentioned.

How you are described

We check that the name, location, services and prices AI tools give for your brand are correct and consistent.

Who appears instead

We list the competitors and sources AI tools use in place of your brand, so you can see where the gap is.

Your website and profiles

We review answer-first content, structured data and profile consistency, the signals AI tools use to verify a brand.

What you get

  • A written report: what each AI tool says about your brand today.
  • A competitor and source view: who is mentioned instead of you and which sources are cited.
  • A prioritised fix list: the changes most likely to make your brand clearer to AI tools, in order.
  • An honest baseline: a record you can compare against when your brand is tested again after the changes.

We do not promise rankings or AI mentions, because no one can. The audit shows where you stand and what is within your control. See a real sample audit.
